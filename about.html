Profile Overview

Background, focus areas, and the path leading into a Cybersecurity degree.

I'm a Coimbatore-based cybersecurity student with a growing focus on network defense, ethical hacking, and digital fraud prevention. Before starting my B.E. in Cybersecurity at CMS College of Engineering and Technology, I've spent time building foundational, hands-on skills — working with tools like Kali Linux, Nmap, Wireshark, and Burp Suite to understand how systems get attacked, and how they're defended.

My interest shows up outside the classroom too: as team lead on a laser-based border intrusion detection concept, in hackathon projects on digital evidence integrity and missing-person identification, and in CheCore — a personal project combining network analysis software with ESP32 hardware.

I hold Fortinet NSE 3 (FCA) and COFPS certifications, along with cybersecurity and AI coursework from UNITAR and HP, and I'm currently working toward CEH certification and an Amateur Radio (HAM) license. I'm also a member of null Kochi, the local chapter of null — The Open Security Community — connecting with practitioners and staying current on real-world security topics.
